1.About this policy

This Privacy Policy describes how the ETracko mobile app (package com.sauravdhoju.expensetracker), developed by Saurav Dhoju, handles information. ETracko is designed so that the developer never receives your financial data. It does not require you to create an account, and there is no ETracko server that your transactions are sent to.

2.Data the app handles

To work as an expense tracker, the app keeps the information you enter or choose to import:

  • Financial records: accounts and their balances, income, expenses, transfers, categories, budgets, bills, recurring payments, savings goals, money lent to others and “forgotten money” entries.
  • Names you type: for example account names, transaction titles and notes, and the names of people you lend money to.
  • Preferences: currency, calendar (AD/BS), theme, reminders, home-screen layout and similar settings.
  • Automation data (only if you enable it): the text of bank SMS messages and payment-app notifications that the app processes, plus the rules it learns (for example, which merchant belongs to which category). See section 4.
ETracko does not collect your contacts, location, photos, device identifiers or advertising ID, and it does not ask for your bank login details.

3.Where your data is stored

All of the data above is stored locally on your device, in the app’s private database. It is not uploaded to the developer or to any ETracko server. The only time data leaves your device is when you choose to:

  • export a backup file and share or save it somewhere yourself, or
  • turn on Google Drive backup (section 5).

Uninstalling the app removes its local database from your phone. Backups you exported or saved to Google Drive remain wherever you put them until you delete them.

4.SMS & notification automation

ETracko can optionally add transactions for you by reading bank SMS messages and notifications from payment apps. This feature is off by default and only runs after you enable it and grant the matching Android permission.

How it works

  • Incoming SMS messages and notifications from the apps you select are checked on your device to see whether they describe a payment or deposit.
  • Reading the amount, matching an account, choosing a category and checking for duplicates all happen offline, on the phone. Message text is never sent to the developer or any server.
  • Messages that are not about money are ignored. Detected transactions are either added automatically or placed in a Review Inbox for you to confirm, depending on your settings.
  • The app keeps a copy of the text of processed messages in a local detection history, so it can avoid duplicates and show you why a transaction was created.

Your controls

  • Turn automation, SMS reading or notification reading off at any time in Settings → Automation.
  • Choose exactly which apps’ notifications may be read.
  • Delete stored message text with Settings → Privacy → Clear detection history, and learned rules with Clear automation rules.
  • Revoke the SMS or notification-access permission in Android settings whenever you like.

5.Google Drive backup

Cloud backup is optional and off by default. If you turn it on, you sign in with your Google account and ETracko uploads a backup file of your app data to your own Google Drive, on the schedule you choose. The developer cannot access your Drive or your backups.

  • ETracko requests only the drive.file permission. This lets the app create and read the backup files it made itself. It cannot see, open or change any other files in your Drive.
  • The sign-in token is stored in your device’s secure storage and is used only to upload and restore your backups.
  • You can turn backup off in Settings → Cloud backup, delete the backup files from your Drive, and remove ETracko’s access at any time from your Google Account’s security settings.
ETracko’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google user data is used only to provide the backup feature you turned on. It is not used for advertising, not sold, and not read by people.

6.Permissions

The app asks for Android permissions only for the features that need them:

Permission Why it is used
Receive SMS To detect bank transaction messages, only when SMS automation is enabled.
Notification access To read notifications from the payment apps you select, only when notification automation is enabled.
Notifications To show your own reminders: daily tracking, bill due dates, budget alerts and loan reminders.
Biometrics To unlock the app with your fingerprint or face if you enable app lock. Biometric data is handled by Android; the app never receives it.
Files & storage To save exported backups and statements, and to open a backup file you pick for restoring.
Internet Only for signing in to Google and uploading or restoring Google Drive backups.

7.Sharing & third parties

The developer does not sell, rent or share your data, and the app contains no advertising, analytics or crash-reporting services. Data reaches a third party only when you choose it:

  • Google, if you enable Google Drive backup (governed by Google’s own privacy policy).
  • Any app or person you share an export with, for example when you send a CSV statement or JSON backup through the share sheet.

8.Security

Your data is kept in the app’s private storage, which other apps cannot read. You can add a PIN and fingerprint or face unlock in Settings → PIN & biometric lock, and hide amounts on screen with one tap. Sign-in tokens are kept in Android’s secure storage.

Exported backup files are not encrypted. Keep them somewhere private, and protect your phone with a screen lock.

9.Your choices & deletion

Because your data is on your device, you control it directly from the app:

  • Export a full JSON backup or CSV statements at any time.
  • Edit or delete any individual entry.
  • Clear detection history and automation rules in Settings → Privacy.
  • Delete all data in Settings → Privacy permanently erases every account, transaction, budget, goal and rule on the device.
  • Uninstalling the app also removes its local data.

Since the developer holds no copy of your data, there is nothing for us to delete on our side. Backups in your Google Drive can be deleted from Drive directly.

10.Children

ETracko is a general personal-finance tool and is not directed at children under 13. The app does not knowingly collect information from children, and it sends no personal information to the developer.

11.Changes to this policy

If the way the app handles data changes, this page will be updated and the effective date at the top will change. Significant changes will also be noted in the app’s release notes.
